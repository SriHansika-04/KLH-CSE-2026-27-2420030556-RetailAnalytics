INTERPRETATION
--------------
The comparative analysis evaluates multiple machine-learning, deep-learning and hybrid configurations using the recorded experimental results.

The proposed model is the XGBoost + LSTM Feature Hybrid. Its performance is compared with standalone XGBoost and alternative hybrid configurations.

The improvement should be interpreted as a measured experimental difference rather than as a claim of a large performance gain.

Note: LSTM and GRU standalone models were evaluated at the monthly level, whereas the transaction-level ML and hybrid models use transaction-level evaluation. Therefore, cross-level comparisons should be interpreted carefully.
