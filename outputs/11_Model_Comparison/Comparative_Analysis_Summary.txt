INTERPRETATION
--------------
The comparative analysis evaluates multiple machine-learning, deep-learning and hybrid configurations using the experimental results generated during model evaluation.

The proposed model is the XGBoost + LSTM Feature Hybrid. Its performance is compared with standalone XGBoost and alternative hybrid configurations.

For regression evaluation, higher R2 indicates better explanatory performance, while lower MAE, RMSE and MAPE indicate lower prediction error.

The magnitude of improvement is reported as the observed experimental difference and should not be interpreted as a claim of a large improvement unless supported by the measured results.

IMPORTANT COMPARABILITY NOTE
-----------------------------
The standalone LSTM and GRU models were evaluated at the monthly level, whereas the transaction-level ML and hybrid models use transaction-level evaluation. Therefore, their metric values should be interpreted with the evaluation level in mind.
